FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I keep my current WhatsApp number?

Yes. We connect your existing business number to the official WhatsApp Business API, so your clients keep messaging the number they already have. Note that once a number is on the API, it is used through the inbox instead of the normal WhatsApp app on a phone.

Can I connect more than one number?

Yes. You can add several numbers, even if they belong to different Meta business accounts. All chats arrive in one inbox, and each chat shows which number it came in on.

How are chats shared between agents?

You choose: each agent gets a set number of chats in turn (for example 2 each), or each new chat goes to whoever has the fewest open chats. Chats go to the right department first, agents who are online come first, and nobody goes over their chat limit. Managers can move any chat by hand.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes, there is an Android app for agents with push alerts, so new chats reach them even when the app is closed. The web version works on any computer and on iPhone browsers.

Is it safe? Who can see the chats?

Agents only see the chats assigned to them. Managers and admins can see all chats. Everyone signs in with their own account, and you can switch an agent off at any time.

Do I need a developer to set it up?

No. We do the setup with you, usually in a day. After that, admins change bot messages, quick replies, departments and working hours from the settings screen.

What does WhatsApp itself charge?

Replying to a client within 24 hours of their last message is free. Template messages, like broadcasts or messages after 24 hours, are charged by Meta per message, at rates that depend on the country. We'll walk you through it.
